From the Love Begins

A personal 2020s Bollywood playlist — the songs that defined late nights, long drives, and quiet moments. Press play, close your eyes, and let the music take you back.

What is this?

A personal ambient radio of curated 2020s Bollywood songs — streams free via YouTube, no sign-up needed.

How does it work?

Songs play in order from a YouTube playlist. Use the controls to play, pause, or skip. Toggle the rain button for lo-fi ambience.

Keyboard shortcuts?

Space = Play/Pause · ← → = Prev/Next · ↑ ↓ = Volume · F = Fullscreen · R = Rain · S = Share card
